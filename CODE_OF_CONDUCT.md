# Be nice to each other

We are here to build useful software and do careful science.

- Treat people with basic courtesy. Criticize ideas, code and evidence without personal attacks.
- Disagreement is welcome. Explain your reasoning, listen to replies, and leave room to be wrong.
- No harassment, threats, spam or publishing someone's private information.
- Respect people's time. Keep discussions relevant and accept a maintainer's decision to close a discussion or decline a change.

Maintainers may remove disruptive content or restrict participation when needed. Apply the same standards to everyone, including maintainers. For a concern, contact the maintainer through the contact options on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/sprajs); do not post private details in a public issue.

That's it: be decent, be curious, and help make the work better.
