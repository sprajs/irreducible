# Correlated proper calibration marginalization (native C++)

The question is the normalized density of observed residuals at a fixed forward-model prediction when several calibration coordinates have a correlated proper prior. For fixed row-major response X, beta ~ N(m,S), epsilon ~ N(0,C), and explicitly declared independence, r = X beta + epsilon implies r ~ N(Xm,K), K = C + X S X^T. The density is with respect to the declared product of residual-coordinate measures. This calculation integrates the calibration coordinates; it neither fits the forward model nor returns a posterior, model evidence, ladder solution or scientific identifiability claim.

`irred/correlated_calibration.hpp` supplies a move-only retained native owner. Parameter order, units, prior identity, response identity, calibration/dependence declarations and measure identity are explicit. X columns have residual-unit/parameter-unit dimensions; S axes have the products of parameter units. Labels do not perform physical unit conversions. Exact row order and measure must match the source Gaussian. p >= 2 and full proper positive-definite S are required. Zero/improper priors and prior-bearing source Gaussians are rejected. Rank-deficient or zero X is allowed: proper prior directions with no response integrate to one.

Preparation preserves the caller source on failure and consumes it only after every candidate allocation and check succeeds. The source owner is moved without copying its covariance/factor, and remains available through `source()` for comparisons with the conditional noise density and exact source selection/provenance inspection. Effective K is necessarily new; source metadata and original matrix-validation history are never overwritten as an independent raw observation. The separate prior record remains inspectable. Preparation retains the effective Gaussian factor and mean shift; evaluation centers residuals and uses that factor without covariance copies or refactorization.

Both arithmetic environments supported by the existing Gaussian are admitted, subject to round-to-nearest and long double with at least 64 mantissa bits and exponent range 16384. Input/output nonzero numbers must be normal binary64. No jitter, regularization, dropped axes or silent prior repair is used. Prior conditioning must satisfy the caller sensitivity budget. Effective covariance preparation uses existing Gaussian admission. Wide products/sums precede binary64 covariance and mean storage. The retained effective factor supplies its condition estimate for rounding diagnostics without duplicate factorization.

Accumulation error estimates use (4p²+4) epsilon_longdouble times absolute accumulated terms, plus measured final binary64 cast error. The covariance row-sum estimate gives eta = estimated ||K^-1||inf times ||delta K||inf; eta >= .01 is withheld. Evaluation additionally withholds when estimated quadratic, log-determinant or log-density perturbations exceed maximum_forward_sensitivity times (1 + absolute reported quantity). These are conservative arithmetic estimates using a condition estimate, not rigorous interval certification. Extreme cancellation/conditioning may therefore remain unresolved. Successful normalized density is numerical acceptance, not inference qualification.

Element limits admit n², np and p² before numeric scans or allocations. The conservative simultaneous preparation payload contains the retained source, candidate Gaussian preparation, input prior capacities, response/shift storage, new covariance, and prior factor and condition scratch. Evaluation separately admits centered residual and Gaussian solve scratch. Payload bounds exclude borrowed input, allocator overhead and RSS. The base and effective factors coexist for the explicit comparison consumer. No performance advantage is claimed.

Owner controls freeze 2e-12 (1+|reference|) per quadratic, log determinant and log density for named well-conditioned small controls before execution. The correlated 2x2 reference uses explicit long-double cofactors, independently of production factorization. Diagonal-prior agreement with sequential `proper_offset` is a compatibility control with shared ancestry, not independent evidence. Null response, rank-deficient response, prior correlation effects, moves and failed-input source preservation are permanent controls. Independent numerical prior integration, permutations and unit Jacobian controls are separately owned. Their evidence qualifies the named synthetic cases only.
