## Scientific assessment

The central gap is a source-defined observational model exercised across its
relevant parameter domain, with identifiable parameters and an accepted
likelihood. Many useful physical and statistical ingredients exist. Their
composition into a faithful reference posterior and then a defensible joint
multi-probe fit remains work.

A precisely specified flat, six-parameter GR ΛCDM model is the initial working
reference. It is a comparison convention supported by mature source definitions
and available tools, not a claim of cosmological truth or universal consensus.
The existing pinned CLASS and official Planck likelihood bridge can establish
that reference before full native CMB closure exists. Native replacement proceeds
in parallel, with its own physical and numerical gates.

The installed Commander low-TT, SimAll low-EE and Plik-lite TTTEEE combination
is a released primary-CMB target. It differs from full Plik plus the
lensing-reconstruction target behind the quoted Planck parameter table.
Reproducing the former must not be called reproduction of the latter. Lensing
theoretical CMB spectra also does not add a reconstruction likelihood. A new
baseline must define species/neutrino masses, helium/BBN, reionization,
primordial coordinates/pivot, priors and calibration nuisance parameters across
the fitted domain.

Current observational experiments optimize H0 at a fixed Planck-conditioned
shape and separately score Planck. They supply conditional controls, rather
than a six-parameter or joint posterior. Existing reference adapters are frozen
to selected cases and fixed calibration: a new reviewed adapter and experiment
identity are needed for broader inference, while historical routes remain intact.

The recorded `+0.29961458` Planck log-likelihood sensitivity exceeds its `0.2`
allocation at the fixed-w, H0=75 test point. Preserve that failure. Qualify the
chosen ΛCDM fit domain; an excluded alternative's failure is not an automatic
blocker for every baseline evaluation. Extending that alternative requires its
own accepted sensitivity checks. Optimizer bracket width is not a posterior
uncertainty or a solver-accuracy certificate.

## Measurement and candidate boundaries

- Expansion predicts distances and observer mappings, not an intrinsic SN
  spectrum. A spectral-time population or radiative-transfer source, training,
  dust/host effects, calibration, instrument response and selection are required.
  Released calibrated fluxes are a useful first vertical; raw CCD reduction is
  a separate source-supported experiment.
- BAO distances are fitted, fiducial/reconstruction-conditioned compressions.
  Full-shape clustering adds transfer, bias, RSD/AP, windows, estimator,
  nonlinear scale cuts and covariance requirements. Exotic-model applicability
  must be established before reusing a standard compression.
- Strong and weak lensing require different measured-response and population
  contracts. Standard-gravity halo lenses do not automatically apply to modified
  gravity. Adding an NFW or Hernquist potential invalidates the existing
  self-gravitating Plummer distribution function; the combined scene needs a
  new tracer equilibrium/DF or Jeans law.
- A new combination needs one conserved state or action and compatible initial
  conditions. Independently normalized H(z) curves cannot be added. Familiar
  nonlinear prescriptions cannot be transferred to an unusual theory without
  domain evidence.
- Prioritize a faithful reference and supported late-time comparison, one
  mass/light/kinematics vertical, EDE versus NEDE, conservation-qualified
  decay/daughter-scalar models, Chaplygin perturbation choices and directional
  Bianchi/timescape tests. These are research hypotheses, not promoted prospects.
  Keep the DGP self-accelerating ghost concern and existing exponential-slope
  and Gauss–Bonnet analytic rejection screens visible.

The [native campaign](native-model-campaign.md) retains the ten models' precise
contracts and historical production qualification. Neither its finite synthetic
grids nor the new Chaplygin/Plummer-population responses establish an observed fit.
Original-paper access and full-reading limits remain distinct from independent
